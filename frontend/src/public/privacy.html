Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DriveAI ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our car rental platform.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or make a booking, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Booking details, such as pick-up and return dates and the vehicle selected
  • Account credentials (your password is stored securely as a one-way hash and is never visible to us in plain text)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and confirm your bookings
  • Send booking confirmations and related notifications (for example, by email)
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries

How We Protect Your Information

Passwords are hashed before storage. Access to administrative functions is restricted to authorized accounts, and all traffic to this site is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may be shared with service providers strictly to the extent necessary to operate the platform (for example, sending an email notification).

Your Choices

You may update your account information at any time from your profile page, or contact us to request that your data be corrected or removed.
